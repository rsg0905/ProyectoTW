Game Rules (Alquerque)

  • Board & Pieces: The game is played on a 5x5 board. Each player starts with 12 pieces.
  • Movement: Players take turns moving a piece to an adjacent empty spot following the lines. Pieces cannot move backwards to their starting row.
  • Capturing: Captures are made by jumping over an opponent's piece into a straight line. Multiple captures in a single turn are allowed.
  • Huffing Rule (Soplo): If a capture is possible, it is mandatory. If a player makes a different move instead of capturing, the piece that could have captured can be removed from the board from the oponent.
  • End of Game: A player loses if they run out of pieces or have no valid moves left.
